## 3. Issues found but not changed (for you to decide)

1. **The repository cannot be fully re-run.** Six scripts read files from a `raw/` folder that is not committed: `build_baci.py`, `build_comtrade.py`, `switch_screen.py`, `new_sectors.py`, `clean_phase2.py`, `charts_new.py`. Please push the raw extracts, or a script that downloads them.
2. **Japan 2011 still needs Korea monthly** (K-stat or UNIPASS), as your gaps section says. Until then, "other suppliers did not step up within three months" holds for the US and the EU only.
3. **Onion timing.** India was already at 2.5 kt in Nov 2023, after the 40% duty (19 Aug 2023) and the minimum export price (Oct 2023). The 6-month pre-window (Jun–Nov 2023) already includes restrictions. Dating the shock from August, as your threats table suggests, would need a pre-window that ends in July 2023.
4. **Palm oil.** Malaysia rose before the ban (about USD 11m a month in Nov 2021–Feb 2022, 24m in March, 41m in April). Keep this case as robustness only, as you already propose.
5. **Red Sea.** The rejection rests on EU export values in euro with no trend control. A within-product comparison of suppliers that do and do not use Suez would be a fairer test.
6. **Identification checks.** Your `audit/validity_checks_log.txt` shows large pre-trends for wheat (+67%) and onions (−48%). Pre-shock exposure is correlated with pre-shock growth across the six importers (−0.81 for onions, +0.52 for wheat). That supports your own reading: the GCC comparison is "a picture, not a test".
7. **China's monthly coverage has gaps**: 141 of 180 months in 2010–2024, and nothing after Dec 2024.
